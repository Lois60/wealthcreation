You meant it when you said this year would be different.

At the beginning of the year, there were things you wanted to change.

  • Perhaps you wanted to take better care of yourself,
  • make progress in your career,
  • give a personal project a proper chance
  • or build relationships you had been missing.

You wrote those things down because they mattered to you, and you could picture how different your life might feel if you followed through.

Then the demands of everyday life took over.

A goal that felt important in January became something you would return to when work settled down, when you had more time or when you felt ready to start again.

Now another year is approaching, and some of the things you wanted for yourself are still waiting.

What hurts is knowing how much you wanted them.

After a while, it becomes difficult to make another promise to yourself without remembering the ones you have already postponed.

Before you carry that disappointment into 2027, I want you to look more closely at how you have been approaching your goals.

There may be something you need to understand, develop or change that another list of resolutions will not address.

A goal can matter to you and still need a better way forward.

Think about building a house. You begin with a picture of what you want to build, then work out the design, the materials, the skills and the order in which the work needs to happen.

  1. Picture
  2. Design
  3. Materials
  4. Skills
  5. Order

Your goals need that kind of consideration too.

A goal can look impressive on paper without reflecting what you truly want.

It can also require skills you have not developed, more time than you have allowed or more attention than you can give while pursuing everything else at once.

That is why I want us to start with the person behind the goals.

What matters to you?

What kind of life are you trying to create?

Which decisions, habits and abilities would help you move towards it from where you are today?

When you begin answering those questions honestly, you can see what needs your attention.

You can recognise a skill you need to learn, a priority you need to choose or a pattern you need to stop repeating.

Those are practical things you can work on, and that is where this webinar begins.

Here is what we will work through together.

01

Understand yourself before deciding what your life should look like.

Explore what matters to you, what you believe is possible for your life and how your experiences or other people’s expectations may be influencing your choices. Begin separating the goals you truly care about from the ones you feel pressured to pursue.

02

Turn the future you imagine into goals you can work towards.

Learn how to move beyond broad wishes such as wanting to do better or achieve more. Define what progress would actually look like in the areas of your life that matter to you, so you have something specific to work on and review.

03

Identify what you need to develop to support your vision.

Look at the gap between what your goals require and the skills, habits and confidence you currently have. Understand how communication, discipline and the ability to work with people can affect your progress, even when you already know your work well.

04

Give your goals an order and a realistic plan.

Learn how to decide what comes first, what depends on something else and what needs more time. Break your vision into manageable steps and priorities, so you can give each goal the attention it needs without trying to change every part of your life at once.

05

Build a way to keep going when motivation drops.

Work through how to turn your intentions into daily and weekly actions. Learn to build routines and structures that support your goals, protect your attention and make it easier to follow through when you are busy or the initial excitement has passed.

06

Review your progress and make useful adjustments.

Learn how to pay attention to what is happening in your life now, recognise what is helping or holding you back and adjust your approach. Develop a way to stay accountable and continue improving as your circumstances and priorities change.

Come if you are ready to take your own life and plans seriously.

A few things you may want to know.

Is this a physical or virtual event?

It is a virtual event. From Vision to Reality takes place live online, so anyone can join from anywhere across the globe. All you need is a device and a stable internet connection.

Do I need to have my goals figured out before attending?

No. You can come with a clear goal, several competing ideas or a sense that something needs to change without knowing exactly what. Understanding what you want and choosing a direction are part of the work we will do.

Is this only for career or business goals?

The approach applies across your life. You can use it to think more clearly about your career, personal development, relationships, wellbeing or a project that matters to you. The focus is on understanding your vision and learning how to act on it.

Is this a private consultation?

This is a live group webinar where you will learn the principles and frameworks behind the From Vision to Reality approach. A private one-on-one consultation is a separate service.

How will I join the webinar?

Your joining link will be emailed to the address you use at checkout before the event. Check your inbox and spam or promotions folder, and plan to join a few minutes early.

What should I bring?

Have a notebook or journal ready, choose a quiet place and make sure you have a stable internet connection. Most of all, come prepared to reflect honestly and participate.
